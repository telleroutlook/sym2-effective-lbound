# Limitations — M-1 Rewritten

## Scope

- Applies only to non-CM/non-dihedral holomorphic Hecke eigenforms
- Mollifier length X ≤ Q(Π, T)^η with η to be optimized
- t-aspect only (N fixed, T → ∞)

## What is NOT achieved

- No explicit θ, c₀, T₀, δ are computed
- No bridge lemma to c_eff is proved
- The core analytic lemma (GL₃ shifted-convolution) is at the research frontier
- The squarefree approximation lemma is not proved

## Downstream impact

M-1 cannot serve as a premise for M-2 or c_eff until:
1. The twisted GL₃ moment asymptotic is established
2. The mollifier cancellation is proved
3. The bridge lemma I(T) ≥ c₀T ⟹ L(½,Π) > 0 is written
