# Novelty — F-2 v2

## What is new in v2 (correcting v1)

1. **Corrected integral definition**: N(A)\GL₂(A) with Φ(e₂ g), not
   N(A)G(Q)\G(A) with Φ(g). These are different objects (unfolded vs automorphic).

2. **Corrected Adjoint Euler factor**: From single factor (1-αβ⁻¹x)⁻¹ to
   correct 3-factor [(1-x)(1-αβ⁻¹x)(1-βα⁻¹x)]⁻¹. This was a load-bearing error.

3. **Corrected citation**: JS81 is Am. J. Math. 103(3) (1981), 499–558, NOT
   Ann. Math. 114 (1981), 459–512.

4. **Added pure-tensor hypothesis**: Factorization ∏_v Ψ_v requires W = ⊗_v W_v.

5. **Corrected archimedean degree**: Z_∞ now has degree 4 (includes ζ_∞), not 3.
   This gives Z_∞(1) = 2π^{-k-1} Γ(k) with the π^{-1} factor.

6. **Fixed uniformity argument**: Nonvanishing from explicit formulas, then
   continuity+compactness gives min > 0. Not continuity alone.

7. **Separated c_loc from C_global**: Product bound C(N₀) defined correctly.

## What is NOT new

- F-2A diagonal positivity argument (established in v1, just definition fixed)
- The factorization L(s, π × π̃) = ζ(s) · L(s, π, Ad)
- The local newvector theory (Casselman 1974)
